#About Me

Hello! My name is Lexie Cox and I am a mechanical engineering major at UNC Charlotte. This page will be used to show my work and progress throughout my time in MEGR 2157. I also hope to showcase my skills and communicate with others through this page. 

When I was asked what I wanted to be when I grew up I had a new answer every time. Ranging from things such as a veterinarian to a singer. However, that all changed when I was in 6th grade and was told about a new program at my school that would do competitive robotics. After being introduced to the world of engineering and the process I was set on on it and continued to do robotics all the way until I graduated and got to experience so many new things and places. I have aspirations to go into biomedical engineering to do research on neuro stimulators and other prosthetics. I have recently been in the works to try and be an intern or an assistant of a team that is doing a clinical trial of a new cancer treatment. I aspire to be able to help people through engineering and use my skills to do so.
