## Analyze
Task A - Portfolio Analysis

**Portfolio 1:**
https://nhoong.github.io/

**A - Navigability)** All of the projects and work experience can be seen in under 30 seconds. There are separate tabs for both his work experience and his most important projects. The projects have little descriptions of what they are as well as the objective and result. They also have very detailed pictures which is more helpful than a detailed description. In his work experience he shows the logo of where he worked as well as what he did and accomplished at the job.

**B - Reproducibility)** I feel that certain projects could be reciprocated, especially those with a full report attached. Other projects could show different angles of the specific parts created. For example, the shock top assembly project shows the individual pieces, their dimensions, and the completed assembly both put together and exploded. 

**C - Evidence of Reasoning)** The portfolio does not show how or why decisions were made only the objective and the result. However, the certain projects that has the full report does show their process. 

**D - Professional Tone)** This portfolio has very professional tone, it is very concise without leaving out important bits. It also uses language that is appropriate for a portfolio and to be given to an employer. 


**Portfolio 2:**
(https://johnduncanstevens.wixsite.com/portfolio/engineeringprojects)

**A - Navigability)** In this portfolio all projects can be accessed within 60 seconds with all of the projects listed in one tab. They all have a picture with a rundown of what the project is. All of the projects have extended details of what the project is, the components, and steps it took to complete each project. 

**B - Reproducibility)** They also show a decision matrix which I find very important in the replication so they are not guessing why they are doing a certain step. There are also very thought out sketches and 3D print examples. I feel that all of these projects can be reciprocated. 

**C - Evidence of Reasoning)** This portfolio shows a very detailed on why he did certain things. Especially the Covid-19 PSA Rube Goldberg Machine, it also shows other peoples thought process and how that influenced their decision. 

**D - Professional Tone)** It does use a very professional tone, as well as the layout is professional. The language is not too complicated to which an average person can't understand it but it is still something that can be presented in a setting that has more specialized people.
