## Communicate

As said above I used the stress analysis calculations from assignment 5 to create the parameters for the CAD model. One parameter it controlled was the diameter of the pin. For my calculations I assumed the length was 1 inch and that was crucial in finding the diameter because I needed to find the maximum moment from the stress calculations. The diameter of the pin was 0.424 inches. If I changed the parameters or put them in the wrong spot the design would not look right and it was hard to go back. At one point during designing my SolidWorks ended up crashing and I had to restart my design process. 

There are tight tolerances and loose tolerances. Tight tolerances are important for something such as a pin so that the fit is correct. A loose tolerance is best for a part that doesn't need to interact with another part or an outside boundary. For example in my design the pin part has a tight tolerance but the connecting piece has a loose tolerance. If there is a non-critical tight tolerance it could have a higher fail rate and the cost would increase because of the need to make more parts. The part could need grinding or another process to allow it to fit costing more money and time. 

This assignment took me roughly 7 hours to complete.
